# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the Federated Ads Protocol. The project is at an early stage: it is currently run by two individual contributors, Suneesh Rajan and Arjun Krishna, working with an AI assistant, and the aim is to bring in co-editors, implementers and reviewers from publishers, advertisers, civil society and standards bodies.

## How to contribute

- **Issues** are for specific problems: errors, unclear text, security or privacy concerns, missing considerations. Please give the section number.
- **Discussions** are for broader questions: whether the approach is right, governance, adoption.
- **Pull requests** are welcome for corrections and proposed text. For substantive changes, open an issue first so the change can be discussed.
- **Security issues:** please report privately to suneeshtr@gmail.com rather than opening a public issue.

## Process

Proposals follow a simple path:

1. Proposal (issue)
2. Discussion
3. Editor's draft (pull request)
4. Last call (at least 14 days)

Decisions and their rationale are recorded in the issue or pull request. Breaking changes to the protocol require at least 90 days' notice once implementations exist. See §23 of the whitepaper.

4. Contributions to the Internet-Draft are subject to the IETF Trust Legal Provisions and the IPR disclosure rules of BCP 78 and BCP 79.

## AI-assisted contributions

AI-assisted contributions are welcome if you disclose them in the pull request and take responsibility for their accuracy. The project's own drafts disclose that they were written with Claude (Anthropic).

## Conduct

Be respectful and constructive, and assume good faith. Criticism of ideas is welcome; personal attacks are not. The [W3C Code of Conduct](https://www.w3.org/policies/code-of-conduct/) applies; see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) for how to report a problem.

## Competition law

Do not share company-specific pricing, rate cards, forward-looking commercial plans or other competitively sensitive information in project channels.
