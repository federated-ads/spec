# Federated Ads brand kit

The logo, icons, badges, social images and design tokens for the Federated Ads Protocol, with guidance on using them. Everything here is a **draft** (October 2026), like the protocol itself.

Every file in this folder except this README is generated by [`scripts/build_brand.py`](../../scripts/build_brand.py). To change the artwork, change the script and rebuild; do not edit the generated files by hand (see [Changing the kit](#changing-the-kit)).

## The mark

![The Federated Ads symbol: three dots evenly spaced on a broken ring, with an empty centre](logo/federated-ads-symbol-colour.svg)

Three peers sit on one ring: publishers, advertisers and the people who see the ads. Nothing sits in the middle. The empty centre is the point: the protocol has no mandatory central server, exchange or registry, and intermediaries are optional.

The mark is drawn on a 32-unit grid: a ring of radius 10 broken into three arcs (stroke 2.5, round caps), with three nodes of radius 3.5 at the top and at the lower left and right. The wordmark is "Federated Ads" in Schibsted Grotesk ExtraBold, tracked at −0.02 em and converted to outlines.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep at least one node's diameter (7 units on the 32-unit grid; 14 px when the symbol is 64 px) clear on every side of the symbol or lockup.
- **Minimum size:** symbol 16 px; horizontal lockup 112 px wide; stacked lockup 96 px wide. Below 16 px, use the word "Federated Ads" instead.

## Do and don't

**Do**
- Use the files as supplied, at or above the minimum size, with clear space.
- Pick the colourway that fits the background.
- Put the mark next to your own logo with clear separation when you say that your product implements the protocol.

**Don't**
- Recolour, stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or animate the mark.
- Add anything to the centre of the ring.
- Build the mark into your own logo or product name.
- Use the mark to suggest that the Federated Ads Initiative, W3C or the IETF endorses, certifies or partners with you.

## Talking about Federated Ads

Describe the relationship factually. The name follows a verb or preposition; it never becomes part of your product's name.

| Say | Don't say |
|---|---|
| Implements Federated Ads v0 (core profile) | Federated Ads Certified |
| Compatible with Federated Ads | Official Federated Ads partner |
| Implements `draft-rajan-federated-ads-protocol-00` (work in progress) | IETF standard, IETF compliant, W3C approved |
| An ad server for Federated Ads | FederatedAds Pro, Federated Ads Server |

Standards bodies have their own rules:
- The Internet-Draft carries no logo and no brand colour. The IETF's current SVG checker accepts black-and-white artwork only.
- A W3C Community Group report must not use the W3C logo. It uses the Community Group logo and the status line W3C requires.

## Badges

The badges are **self-declared**. Displaying one is your own public claim that your software implements the stated version and profile of the protocol. Nobody reviews or approves it, and no badge is ever required to connect with other nodes.

- Link the badge to your own evidence: test results, or a conformance statement published on your domain.
- Always show the version. Update or remove the badge when you stop meeting it.
- Do not call the badge a certification. "Certified" is reserved until an independent certifier exists.

Use the SVG with alt text that repeats the claim, for example:

```html
<a href="https://adserver.example/federated-ads-tests/">
  <img src="implements-federated-ads-v0-core-light.svg" alt="Implements Federated Ads v0, core profile" width="275" height="76">
</a>
```

## Ad disclosure label

The label that people see on each ad (the "Ad" label and the "Why this ad" explanation) is part of the protocol's behaviour, not of this kit. It is not specified yet; when it is, it will be defined in the whitepaper and the Internet-Draft, and its artwork added here.

## Diagram icons and Mermaid

The icons in [`icons/`](icons/) are 24 × 24, stroke 1.75, round caps and joins, drawn in `currentColor`. Always show a text label with an icon.

For Mermaid diagrams, use the `base` theme with the brand colours:

## Domain

The proposed domain is **federated-ads.org**, on its own, with no redirect domains. It is a proposal: as of 7 October 2026 the name is not registered. Until it is registered and serving the site, the site stays at `https://federated-ads.github.io/spec/`, and the artwork and link previews point there or to the GitHub repository. Do not print or link the new domain anywhere before then.

When the domain is live:
1. Set `SITE_ADDRESS` in [`scripts/build_brand.py`](../../scripts/build_brand.py) to `federated-ads.org` and rebuild.
2. Set the custom domain in the repository's GitHub Pages settings, and update the absolute URLs (`og:url`, `og:image`) in the whitepaper HTML.
3. Use the domain as the project's handle where a platform allows it, for example a Bluesky handle verified by DNS.
4. Record in `docs/governance/` who holds the registration, and that it will pass to a neutral steward if one is formed.

The vocabulary namespace stays at `https://w3id.org/federated-ads/v0`; w3id.org redirects can be pointed at the new site without changing any identifier.

## Provenance

The mark, the lockups and this kit were designed with Claude (Anthropic) on 7 October 2026, under the direction of a human contributor who chose the design. They follow the project's disclosure of AI assistance.

This affects rights in the artwork. The US Copyright Office's January 2025 report concludes that "prompts alone do not provide sufficient human control to make users of an AI system the authors of the output" ([Copyright and Artificial Intelligence, Part 2](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/Copyright-and-Artificial-Intelligence-Part-2-Copyrightability-Report.pdf)). The licences above apply to whatever copyright exists in these files. Before the project registers a trademark or depends on the artwork's copyright, a human designer should review or redraw the final vectors, and anyone who contributes artwork should agree in writing to assign their rights to a future neutral steward.

## Changing the kit

1. Edit the definitions in [`scripts/build_brand.py`](../../scripts/build_brand.py): mark geometry, colours, typography or layouts.
2. Install the tools: `pip install fonttools uharfbuzz pillow`, and the `resvg` renderer (or set `RESVG` to a compatible command).
3. Run `scripts/build_brand.py` and review the PNGs by eye.
4. Open a pull request. Changes to the mark, the name or the badge rules are design decisions for the maintainers, raised as an issue first.
