# Federated Ads Protocol content taxonomy

The Federated Ads content taxonomy (`https://w3id.org/federated-ads/tax/1`) is **not yet published**. It is planned as an open, versioned vocabulary mapped to the IAB Tech Lab Content Taxonomy 3.x and Ad Product Taxonomy 2.0, separating "sensitive" from "unsafe" content (whitepaper section 9).

Follow progress or comment at [github.com/federated-ads/spec/issues](https://github.com/federated-ads/spec/issues).
